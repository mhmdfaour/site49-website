# Image brief: Site49 marketing website (South Africa)

Generate the images below with your image generation tool and save each as a PNG in THIS folder with the exact
file name given. Do nothing else (no code, no other files). When done, write `DONE.md` listing each file and one line
on what it shows.

## What Site49 is (use this to make the images true)
Site49 sells **WebStarter**: the Site49 team builds a 4-page website (Home, About Us, Menu/Products/Portfolio,
Contact Us) for a South African small business for **R49/month, R0 setup**. The owner signs up at app.site49.co.za,
adds business details (name, WhatsApp number, opening hours, address, social links), uploads photos/logo/menu,
approves a private preview (one revision round), pays by **EFT**, and the site goes live at
**yourname.mysite49.co.za** with a big green **WhatsApp** button so customers message the business directly.

Brand look for any app/website screen: white and very light blue (#eaf3ff) UI, navy text (#0b2145), cobalt blue
buttons (#075bee), mint green ticks (#44d596), rounded cards, clean sans-serif. The customer websites themselves can
use the business's own warm colours.

## Audience and creative direction
South African small-business owners: township and suburban, Joburg/Soweto/Durban/Cape Town/Pretoria. Show real,
diverse South Africans (mostly Black, also Coloured, Indian, white) running real local businesses: a kasi café or
shisanyama, a hair/braiding salon, a barber, a plumber or electrician with a bakkie, a home baker, a spaza shop.
Real SA settings: minibus taxis, corrugated-iron and brick shopfronts, jacaranda trees, Highveld light, Table
Mountain only if it fits. Warm, proud, optimistic, documentary-style photography (natural light, shallow depth of
field) — premium but authentic, NOT stocky, NOT generic office people.

**Every image must show the product or a benefit:** a phone, laptop or tablet with the Site49 account or the
customer's new website on screen, or a customer finding/using that website (e.g. tapping WhatsApp). No filler
photos.

## Hard rules
- On-screen text: keep it SHORT, LARGE and correctly spelt. Only use words from this brief. If text would be
  tiny, use simple shapes instead of text.
- Allowed numbers: R49, R0, R149, 01–05 step numbers. NO other prices, counts, ratings, stars, reviews,
  testimonials, follower counts or "#1" claims.
- No real brands or logos (no Google, Apple, Samsung, WhatsApp logo, bank logos, Coca-Cola, etc.). A plain green
  chat-bubble button is fine. Not a real map app.
- No real people, no celebrities. No watermarks. No captions saying "AI".
- Photorealistic unless stated.
