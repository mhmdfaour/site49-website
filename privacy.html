How Site49 handles your personal information.

Who we are

Site49 builds and hosts small business websites in South Africa. The responsible party for your personal information is [LEGAL NAME — pending CIPC], trading as Site49, Johannesburg, South Africa.

Email: info@site49.co.za · Phone / WhatsApp: +27 78 383 8383

Our Information Officer is [INFORMATION OFFICER NAME] (the owner of Site49). Contact the Information Officer at info@site49.co.za.

What we collect

  • Your account email address.
  • Your business details: name, what you do, contact details, hours, and the text for your website.
  • Payment references from Paystack. Your card details go to Paystack and never reach Site49.
  • Files you upload, such as your logo and photos.

Why we collect it

We use this information to create and run your account, build and host your website, take and confirm payments, keep billing records, contact you about your site, and meet our legal duties. We do not sell your information.

How long we keep it

  • Account data and your website: for as long as your account exists.
  • If a subscription stays unpaid for 2 months, your site goes offline and we send you a notice. If it is still unpaid 30 days after the notice, your website, its files and your uploads are deleted permanently.
  • Billing records: 5 years, as the law requires.

Who processes your information

  • Supabase (Ireland): account data.
  • Cloudflare (Western Europe): website files and hosting.
  • Paystack: payments.
  • HostAfrica / HMailPlus: email.
  • GitHub: hosts this marketing site.

Cross-border storage. Site49 stores account data with Supabase in Ireland (EU) and website files with Cloudflare in Western Europe. Both are subject to the EU’s GDPR, which gives protection substantially similar to POPIA (section 72). Payments are handled by Paystack; email by HostAfrica.

Google Maps on customer websites

If a business shows a Google map on its Site49 website, Google may set cookies in your browser when the map loads; see Google’s privacy policy (opens in a new tab).

Your rights

Under POPIA you may:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you (access);
  • ask us to correct it;
  • ask us to delete it;
  • object to how we use it;
  • complain to the Information Regulator at inforegulator.org.za (opens in a new tab).

How to make a request

Email info@site49.co.za from the address on your account, or message us on WhatsApp. Tell us what you need. We may ask you to confirm who you are. Deleting your information also removes your website, and billing records we must keep by law are kept for 5 years.

Changes

We may update this notice. The current version is always on this page.
